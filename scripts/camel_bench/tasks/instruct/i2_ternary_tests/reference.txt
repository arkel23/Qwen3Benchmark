\textbf{Populations and tests.} Three populations are compared. The first is the selection split, where the best checkpoint is chosen. The second is the FLEURS test set \cite{conneau2022fleurs}, available for \NumFleursLocales{} of the locales. The third is the in-domain WorldSpeech test set. Each ternary arm is paired with the FP16 arm per locale. We report the mean and median paired difference and a bootstrap 95\% confidence interval on the mean. We also report a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank $p$ \cite{wilcoxon_individual_1945} and a Holm correction across the four arms \cite{holm_simple_1979}. Finally, we report the minimum detectable effect (MDE) at 80\% power. A positive difference means the ternary arm is worse.
